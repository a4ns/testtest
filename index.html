    Handling

    Keyboard

    Click a key to change it, then press the new key. Backspace clears it. Esc/P pause and R restarts.

    Touch

    Touch controls

    Gamepads work too: D-pad moves, A/B rotate, Y rotates 180°, bumpers hold, Start pauses.

    Soundtrack

    Everything you hear is synthesized live in your browser. The music speeds up as you level up.

    How to play

    • Drag sidewaysMoves the piece one column per step
    • TapRight half rotates clockwise, left half counter-clockwise
    • Drag downSoft drop
    • Flick downHard drop
    • Flick upHold the piece

    Prefer buttons? Switch in Settings → Controls.

    Fill a row from wall to wall to clear it. Clearing four rows at once is a Tetris (800 × level). Singles, doubles and triples score 100, 300 and 500 × level.

    T-Spins: rotate a T piece into a tight slot as its last move. A T-Spin Double scores 1,200 × level, a Triple 1,600.

    Back-to-Back: chain Tetrises and T-Spins without a plain clear in between for a 1.5× bonus. Combos: clear lines with consecutive pieces for +50 × combo × level.

    Perfect Clear: empty the whole field for a huge bonus. Hard drops earn 2 points per row, soft drops 1.

    Hold a piece for later, once per drop. The ghost shows where the piece will land.

    Daily Challenge: a new two-minute run every day with the same pieces for everyone. Play on consecutive days to build a streak. Sprint shows your pace against your best at every 10 lines.
